About

I am a researcher at Tencent Ads (Tencent), based in Shenzhen, China. My work focuses on applying large language models to user understanding and large-scale personalized systems, with an emphasis on turning behavioral data into compact, useful representations for recommendation and advertising.

I am interested in practical AI systems that connect model capability, representation quality, and production efficiency.

Research Interests

  • Large Language Models for User Modeling and Behavioral Understanding
  • Long-Term User Interest, Memory, and Behavior Representation
  • Recommender Systems, Computational Advertising, and Personalization
  • Efficient LLM Inference, Representation Compression, and AI Systems at Scale

Selected Publications

KuaFu: Compressing Long User Behavior into Understanding at Billion Scale

Jiahao Hui, Lin Zhu, Yishen Hu, Jingdong Shu, Zetai Jiang, Xining Ran, Ben Tan, Yeshou Cai, Gong Chen, Haijie Gu, Jie Jiang.

Preprint, 2026 · arXiv:2609.31045 · cs.IR, cs.CL, cs.LG

A unified behavior-compression layer for converting long user behavior histories into compact representations for large-scale user understanding. The work explores item-level compression, fidelity-oriented training, and evaluation across production profiling tasks.
